Forecast limitations
  • No overlapping labels: each forecast targets exactly one subsequent trading day. Volatility clustering still induces dependence; a paired 20-day block bootstrap is descriptive, not proof of significance.
  • The tree is selected on 2019–2020 validation QLIKE, refit through observed 2020 labels and frozen. GARCH parameters refit every 21 trading days; rolling and EWMA states update daily. No test-based tuning.
  • Features contain information through the forecast close only. Target scaling by 10,000 is numerical; no preprocessing is fit to test data.
  • Permutation importance uses validation data; correlated volatility features share information, shuffling disrupts temporal structure, and importance is neither causal nor an independent test.
  • A single squared return is a noisy variance proxy. Volatility MAE compares annualized forecast volatility to annualized absolute daily return, not to observable true volatility.
  • This SPY experiment does not validate forecasts or VaR for the five-basket portfolio. The tree cannot extrapolate beyond its training leaf averages.
  • AI loading is SMH proxy exposure, not machine learning. This decision tree is the separate ML experiment.

05 / Evidence & assumptions

A dated research demonstration.

Report date: 2026-10-12 · Price cutoff: 2026-09-30 · Weights: 2026-10-01 · Basket formation: 2026-09-30.

Source: Yahoo Finance via yfinance, adjusted close (auto_adjust=True). Pulled at 2026-10-04 01:36 UTC. Inputs are frozen, not live prices.

  • Demonstration thresholds are illustrative controls, not regulatory limits or institutional risk appetite.
  • Current S&P 100 membership and 1 October 2026 OEF weights are projected backward. Survivorship bias and historical-weight bias remain.
  • Covariance uses the original quarterly characteristic-basket log returns. Those changing groups are a proxy for current basket risk, not a fixed-holdings return history.
  • Volatility and 10-day 99% VaR/ES reuse the study's zero-mean normal, square-root-time approximation on weighted basket log returns. Positive loss magnitudes shown here are linear approximations, not exact compounded simple-return tail losses.
  • Hypothetical shocks are end-to-end simple returns: portfolio P&L / NAV = sum(weight × shock). They are scenarios, not forecasts; no time horizon or probability is assigned.
  • Historical replay holds today's constituents and initial weights fixed, applies adjusted-price changes between exact endpoints, then sums P&L by today's baskets. It is a hindsight exposure replay, not actual historical fund performance.
  • No derivatives repricing, liquidity liquidation model, transaction costs, funding constraints or counterparty model is implemented. Equity price stress is not a liquidity-risk measurement.
  • SPY forecast evaluation is separate from basket risk measurement. AI loading is semiconductor-proxy exposure, not an ML model.
